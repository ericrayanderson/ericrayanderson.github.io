To-scale layout

Budget mat swap

Swap the Country Club Elite for a Golfguru 5×5 mat at $159.99 to free up budget for a better display later.

Display

Use a tablet or cast to a TV for now. A short-throw projector like the BenQ TH671ST (about $850–$950 new) fits the room but is a later upgrade.

Room prep checklist

  • Remove the lumber rack and clear the back wall for the screen
  • Pad the drain pipe in the back-right corner, or keep it outside the cage
  • Relocate the hanging cord on the left wall
  • Cover the ceiling above the hitting area with netting, plywood, or drywall
  • Tape out tee and mat spots and take slow driver test swings (tall players up to 6′6″ may be limited to irons at 9.5 ft)
