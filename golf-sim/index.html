At a glance

If any left-handed golfer will play, choose Setup B (Garmin R10). If the bay is for right-handed golfers only, choose Setup A (Square Golf HE). Both fit a 12 × 14 ft room with a 9.5 ft ceiling once you clear the back wall and protect the ceiling above the hitting spot.

Who can swing a driver here

Rule of thumb: a driver needs golfer height plus about 3.5 to 4 ft. With a 9.5 ft ceiling (measured at the lowest point), max golfer height is about 5′10″ to 6′0″ with a normal swing.

Taller players (up to 6′6″) should test swing slowly, choke down, or use irons and hybrids.

Estimate only. Tape an alignment stick to a driver and try the exact hitting spot before you buy.

Room prep checklist

  • Remove the lumber rack and clear the back wall for the screen
  • Pad the drain pipe in the back-right corner, or keep it outside the cage
  • Relocate the hanging cord on the left wall
  • Cover the ceiling above the hitting area with netting, plywood, or drywall
  • Tape out tee and mat spots and take slow driver test swings (tall players up to 6′6″ may be limited to irons at 9.5 ft)
8

Jargon explained

Launch monitor
A small device that watches the ball (and sometimes the club) and shows distance, speed, and direction on a phone, tablet, or TV.
Impact screen
The white fabric you hit into. It stops the ball and can show a course image if you use a projector.
Enclosure (cage)
The netting frame around the screen that keeps mishits from flying into the room.
Radar vs camera
Radar units (like the Garmin R10) sit behind you and bounce radio waves off the ball. Camera units (like Square Golf HE or SkyTrak) sit beside the ball and film it. Both work; they just need different space.
Subscription
A yearly fee some brands charge to unlock full course play or cloud features. Setup A does not require one; Setup B uses Garmin Golf at $99.99/yr.

Next steps

  1. Clear the roomTake down the lumber rack, move crates, and free the back wall for the screen and cage.
  2. Measure the lowest pointConfirm the 9.5 ft height at the insulation, joists, and drain pipe, not at the open joist bays.
  3. Test swing in placeTape out the mat and tee spots. Take slow driver swings. Decide if taller players will use irons only.
  4. Pick Setup A or B and orderRighties only → Setup A. Any lefty → Setup B. Order the monitor, cage, and mat together.
  5. Install and protectSet the cage against the back wall, pad the drain pipe, cover the ceiling above the hitting area, then place the mat and launch monitor.
